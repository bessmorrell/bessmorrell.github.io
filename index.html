Bess Morrell
Organist in central North Carolina
Bess Morrell is an organ student of Dr. Timothy Olsen at University of North Carolina School of the Arts. She specializes in sacred music and experiments with merging new genres and sounds to her ancient instrument. She plays in both liturgical and concert settings, and entices listeners with her creative grasp on harmony.

Bess also has a B.S. in Computer Science and works as a Data Analyst for JF Kilfoil.
